L-system Camera draws the live camera image as a single continuous fractal curve. Dark parts of the picture become thick line segments, bright parts thin ones, so the whole scene is rendered with one unbroken space-filling line.

Features:
- 18 curves to choose from — Hilbert, Moore, Gosper, Peano, Dragon, Sierpinski and more — picked from an icon strip
- One big slider for contrast, brightness or complexity
- Shutter button holds the frame so you can share exactly what you see; tap resume to go live again
- Switch between the back and front camera
- Share the result as a PNG to any app
- Works in portrait and landscape

The app needs camera access to work. Nothing is stored, nothing is uploaded, and there are no accounts, ads or analytics.
