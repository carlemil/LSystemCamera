# Privacy Policy — L-system Camera

**Last updated: 2026-09-03**

L-system Camera collects no personal data, transmits no data over the network,
and contains no third-party SDKs, analytics, advertising, or tracking.

## Camera

The app requests the camera permission because it draws what the camera sees as
a fractal curve, live. Camera frames are processed in memory on your device and
are discarded immediately after each frame is drawn. No photo or video is ever
stored by the app or sent anywhere.

## Sharing

If you tap Share, the current drawing is written to the app's private cache
folder and handed to the app you pick in the system share sheet. Nothing is
shared unless you choose to share it, and only with the app you choose.

## Permissions

The only permission the app requests is the camera. It requests no network,
storage, location, contacts, or microphone access.
